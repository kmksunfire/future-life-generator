Skywing River Run

Pick a course and fly it end to end. Your goal: collect all 15 gold rings (they line up on the left). Glowing wind swirls give a speed gust. Skim low over the water for bonus points, fly through the gold rings and the waterfalls, thread the sea arches, and steer clear of the rock pillars. Land on the deck at the end.

📱 Tilt to play: tip left and right to steer, tip back to climb, tip forward to dive. Flick the phone forward for a wing beat. Swipe for tricks: up for a loop, sideways for a barrel roll, curl a quarter circle to swoop. Tap 🤸 Moves for secrets.

Touch anywhere: hold left or right to steer, swipe down to climb, swipe up to dive. Wing beat gives a burst of speed. Phone tilt works too (turn it on in the top bar). Keyboard: arrows to fly, space for a wing beat.

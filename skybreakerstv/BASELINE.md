# Sky Breakers TV: November 5 baseline

This folder is the locked-in version of skybreakerstv.com from November 5, 2026: the solid build we branch off from and always come back to.

What's in it:
- Home page with the "Pick your ride" picture menu and MKE Creatives links
- Flight Lab: paper experiments, games, and Da Vinci's flying machines (real wooden kits)
- Trail Run: Pedal Kart, Kiteboard, The Iron Claw; one ride + course menu (no garage); rival racers; kite S-curve dance; secret board wings (counter-clockwise spin, then clockwise) with the slow-motion reveal
- Skywing River Run: flowing tricks (swipe-speed rolls, corkscrew, dive swing, Flow chains, wing trails), tuning panel at skywing.html?tune=1, bird-style tail, Red Canyon freeze fixed
- Soaring-eagle intro transition, course and ride picture pop-ups
- Inventor's Workbench, ABC page

To restore it: zip the contents of this folder (index.html at the top level) and upload it to Cloudflare Pages (Create deployment, Upload zip).

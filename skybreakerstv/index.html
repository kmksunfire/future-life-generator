    Behind the scenes

    How an episode gets made

    Sky Breakers is made with AI tools, start to finish, by one artist in Milwaukee. Here is the whole process, including the shots that went wrong.

    1. ScriptEvery episode is written and shot-listed, line by line.
    2. StoryboardEach shot is drawn and timed before any art is made.
    3. StillsProduction frames are generated to match the boards.
    4. AnimationStills become moving shots, then get cut together.

    For parents

    What your kids get out of it

    Made for ages 6–10

    Adventure without real danger. The worst that happens is a soggy rope or a stolen sandwich.

    Learning by building

    Every episode has an invention that fails before it works, so kids see that trying again is part of making things.

    Made with AI

    The series is created with AI image and animation tools, and every video is labeled as AI-generated.
